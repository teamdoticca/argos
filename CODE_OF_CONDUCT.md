# Code of Conduct

Argos welcomes participation regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, give actionable technical feedback, and accept that contributors may disagree.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at participants, doxxing, impersonation and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information. These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Doticca maintainer at <fotisgpap@doticca.com>. Include relevant links and context; avoid circulating private reports publicly. Maintainers handle reports as confidentially as reasonably possible, while sharing information when necessary to investigate or protect participants.

Maintainers may remove content, request corrections, restrict participation temporarily or ban participants according to the seriousness and recurrence of the behavior. A person involved in a report should not adjudicate it. Appeals may be sent to the same contact for reconsideration; the project does not promise an independent appeals body or a response SLA.
